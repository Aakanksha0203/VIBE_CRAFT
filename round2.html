How The 3D Free Class Locator Works

Building Stack Navigation & Safe Room Allocation

3D Building Stack Navigation

The 3D model presents the complete campus building structure from Ground Floor up to the 7th Floor. Each floor slab shows live counters of free and occupied rooms.

How to interact: Click any floor slab in the 3D tower to enter that floor. Inside, all classrooms, labs, and seminar halls are displayed in volumetric 3D with uninterrupted countdown timers.

Safe Duration Calculation Formula
Let P_current be the active class period (1 to 8).
Let k be the earliest period where a lecture or lab is scheduled.
Consecutive Free Periods = k - P_current
Safe Duration (Minutes) = Consecutive Free Periods × 50 minutes
Countdown Target = Start Timestamp of Period k
